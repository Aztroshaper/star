SINISTARS

ILLUSTRATION & CONCEPT ART

SYNOPSIS

When a star dies, it should stay dead, but they come back as something monstrous...a Sinistar. Now the cosmos is breaking apart, and Lucía the human teams up with Starthoot, a conscious Sinistar, to find the Star Shaper and stop the chaos before every fallen star rises again.

VISUAL DEVELOPMENT

The development of the Sinistars series explores dark themes and dense atmospheres. The color palette focuses on deep blacks contrasted with flashes of warm lighting. Every stroke is designed to evoke mystery and raw power.

Fanarts

Tributes & Redesigns

STYLIZATION

Reinterpreting iconic characters from pop culture, anime, and video games through a unique stylistic lens. The goal is to maintain the essence of the original designs while applying modern rendering techniques and experimental color palettes.

APPROACH

Each piece is an exercise in dynamic anatomy and lighting studies, seeking to capture epic moments or emotional atmospheres that connect deeply with the respective fan communities worldwide.

OC

Original Characters

WORLD BUILDING

Creation of visual universes from scratch. Each original character (OC) comes with a background story that dictates their costume design, body language, and personal color palette. It is the purest expression of conceptual design.

NARRATIVE

The illustrations do not just show the character, but tell a story about their world. Environmental elements, props, and visual effects combine to bring 100% original creations to life with immersive depth.
